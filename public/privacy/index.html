Your music stays on your device

Effective 28 August 2026

Local storage

Transposing Sketchpad stores your current title, instrument, tempo, and notes in this browser’s IndexedDB. Demo changes use memory only and are discarded when you leave the demo.

Audio and tracking

Audio is synthesized on this device. We do not operate accounts, analytics, advertising, or a cloud score database.

Sharing and export

A share link places a compact sketch after the URL’s #. The app does not upload that fragment. Services you use to send a link may process it. JSON exports are files you control. Imported files are read on this device.

Removing data

Use “Start over” to replace the current sketch. Clear this site’s browser storage to remove local data and cached offline files.

Permissions

Web MIDI is requested only after you choose “Connect MIDI.” The app does not send device or note data to us.
