Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026

Your music stays on your device

Transposing Sketchpad stores your current title, instrument, tempo, and notes in your browser’s IndexedDB. Audio is synthesized on your device. We do not operate an account system, analytics tracker, advertising system, or cloud score database.

Sharing and export

A share link places a compact copy of your sketch after the URL’s #. It is not uploaded by this app, though any service you use to send the link may process it. JSON exports are files you control. Imported files are read locally.

Removing data

Use “Start over” to replace the current sketch, or clear this site’s storage in your browser to remove all local data and cached offline assets.

Permissions

Web MIDI is requested only when you choose “Connect MIDI.” We do not receive device or note data.
